the knobs and buttons

MASTERthe volume
SELECTturns the wheel of the twelve signs; rest on one and it opens
PRESETSstraight to the next sign, or the one before
ALGORITHMthe tips: how to use it, a page a click
OCT−stops the music, and starts it where it stopped
OCT+keeps the four knobs K1 to K4 for next time
K1low pass: turn up to close the piece down to a rumble
K2high pass: turn up to thin it to a whisper
K3echo: a dub echo that feeds on itself
K4reverb: a great hall
FX to RECthe twelve signs, Aries to Pisces, a button each; the playing sign's button is lit

the lower buttons

The bottom left button makes the sign answer: the ram bleats, the lion roars, the water pours.

Every other one is an effect for as long as you hold it, and you can hold several. Let go and the piece is as it was.

Try this: hold Tape stop (count nine along the bottom edge), choose another sign, and let go. The new piece winds up to speed.

by itself

Each piece composes itself as it plays, so it never repeats. After 30 minutes of playing, the next sign takes over: like the 30 degrees each has in the sky. Left alone, it goes round all twelve in six hours.

from outside

The FM-1 also listens to MIDI, by its USB cable or its MIDI socket. Any note makes the sign answer, as the bottom left button does. Program change 0 to 11 chooses the sign, Aries to Pisces.
