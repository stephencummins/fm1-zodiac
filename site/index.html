Twelve signs that play themselves.

Zodiac is a firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1. Each sign is a picture and a piece of music, and each piece composes itself as it goes, so no two playings are alike. Choose a sign and leave it running, or press a key and it answers.

the controls

PRESETthe next sign, or the one before
any keythe sign answers: the ram bleats, the lion roars, the water pours
K1the sign's own knob (its name is under each picture)
K2echo
K3mist
K4light
PLAYstops the music and starts it where it stopped
SAVEkeeps the sign and the knobs for next time

Over MIDI, any note is a key and program change 0 to 11 chooses a sign. Nothing is written on the picture; a screen reader is told the names.

to install

  1. Connect the FM-1 to a computer by USB.
  2. Open the install page in Chrome or Edge and press Install.
  3. Do not unplug the cable while it writes. The FM-1 restarts as Zodiac.

The same page puts M-VAVE's own firmware back. Zodiac replaces the firmware on the FM-1 while it is installed; it is an early version, offered with no warranty.
